How much does a loan really cost?

Put in a loan and see what it takes back: the monthly EMI, the total interest, and how much of every rupee you repay is interest. Interest on a loan is what Islamic finance calls riba — this page shows its real size.

Moin Ali Syed
Founder, Purevesting
Last updated 6 October 2026 · 3 min read

Pick a loan above, or change any number.

The buttons use typical rates, for illustration only — your lender's rate will differ: home loan 8.5% over 20 years; car loan 9.5% over 5; education loan 10.5% over 7; personal loan 13% over 5; credit card dues about 42% a year (3.5% a month) over 2. The EMI is worked out the standard way, with the yearly rate divided by twelve each month; processing fees, insurance and penalties are not included, and they make a loan cost more.

Year by year

In the first year, ₹2,52,709 of the ₹3,12,416 you pay is interest — 81%. An EMI pays the interest first, so the loan itself shrinks slowly at the start.

Why this page looks alarming

Because the cost is real

An EMI looks small next to the price of a house or a car. Added up over the years, the interest can be as large as the loan itself — or larger. The red on this page is that number.

Short and expensive, or long and slow

A credit card or a personal loan charges a high rate for a short time; a home loan a lower rate for a very long time. Try both buttons and compare the meter.
