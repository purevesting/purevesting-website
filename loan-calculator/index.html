In the first year, ₹2,52,709 of the ₹3,12,416 you pay is interest — 81%. An EMI pays the interest first, so the loan itself shrinks slowly at the start.

Read more: the typical rates, and what is left out

The buttons use typical rates; your lender's rate will differ: home loan 8.5% over 20 years; car loan 9.5% over 5; education loan 10.5% over 7; personal loan 13% over 5; credit card dues about 42% a year (3.5% a month) over 2.

The EMI is worked out the standard way, with the yearly rate divided by twelve each month. Processing fees, insurance and penalties are not included, and they make a loan cost more. A real loan's rate can change over its life if it is a floating rate.
