Ask a question, or tell us what to fix

Questions shape what gets covered next — videos, newsletter issues and new pages. Feedback shows what is missing, unclear or wrong on the site. Both arrive in Purevesting's inbox and are read.

Ask a question

About any product, rule or ruling — EPF, a mutual fund, gold, how a Shariah screen works, what purification means for you.

What can't be answered: personal questions like "should I buy this share" or "where should I put my money" — that would be investment advice, and Purevesting is not registered to give it. Good questions may be answered publicly, in a video, the newsletter or on a page, without your name.

Send feedback

Something missing, unclear or broken? Say which page, if you can. A factual mistake gets checked against its source and, if the page is wrong, fixed and recorded in the corrections log.

What happens to what you send

Messages are delivered by email through Web3Forms, a form service. Your email address is optional, is used only to reply to you, and is never sold. If you would rather write directly, the address is salam@purevesting.com.
