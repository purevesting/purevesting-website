Install the Purevesting app

Free, no app store and nothing to update. It opens from your home screen, and pages you've opened before work without internet.

If an Install button doesn't appear here in a few seconds, use your browser's menu instead — the steps for each phone are below.

How to install it

On an Android phone
  1. Open purevesting.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu — the three dots ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app. On some phones it says Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install. The Purevesting icon appears on your home screen.

Other browsers, such as Samsung Internet, have the same option in their menu, usually called Install or Add to Home screen.

On an iPhone or iPad
  1. Open purevesting.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up. In newer versions of Safari it sits inside the ··· menu.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The Purevesting icon appears on your home screen.
Opened this from Instagram or YouTube?

Apps like Instagram and YouTube often open links in their own small browser, which can't install anything. Open the page in your phone's own browser first:

  1. Tap the menu — ⋮ or ···, usually at the top right.
  2. Choose Open in Chrome, Open in browser or Open in Safari — the name depends on the app.
  3. Then follow the steps for your phone above.
On a computer

In Chrome or Edge, look for the install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar, or open the browser's menu and choose Install. On a Mac, Safari can do the same: File, then Add to Dock. Purevesting then opens in its own window, like any other program.

What you get

It opens like an app

From your home screen, full-screen, without the browser's address bar around it.

Pages you've opened work offline

A saved page says so, because figures can change — each one shows its "as of" date.

It is always up to date

Whenever you're online, every page loads fresh from purevesting.com.

It asks for nothing

No account, no sign-in, and no permissions: not your location, contacts, camera or notifications.

To remove it, press and hold the Purevesting icon, then choose Uninstall on Android or Remove on iPhone. Something not working? Write to me at salam@purevesting.com with your phone and browser.
