What that means

Not investment advice

No page, table or calculator is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything.

Read more

Purevesting is not registered with SEBI, the Securities and Exchange Board of India, as a research analyst or an investment adviser. So nothing on this site — no page, table or calculator — is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any share, fund or other product.

Tables list products in a fixed order and never rank them. Personal questions like "should I buy this share?" aren't answered, because answering them would be investment advice. Whether something suits you depends on your own money and plans; a SEBI-registered investment adviser can advise on that.

Shariah answers are quoted, not issued

Quoted from fatwas, Shariah boards and standards — never a ruling by Purevesting.

Read more

Every Shariah answer on the site is quoted from a fatwa, a Shariah board or a screening standard, with a link to it in the page's Sources. None of them is a ruling by Purevesting, and where scholars differ, Purevesting doesn't pick a side.

The screener works the same way: its "Halal" and "Not halal" come from NSE's Nifty500 Shariah index, not from a judgement of Purevesting's own. For your own situation, ask a qualified scholar.

The calculators are illustrations

They show what your numbers would lead to: not forecasts or quotes.

Read more

The SIP, loan and purification calculators show what the numbers you type in would lead to. They are illustrations, not forecasts or quotes: real investments don't grow at a steady rate, a real loan's cost depends on the lender's terms, and scholars differ on how to work out purification. A number a calculator starts on is there to begin from, not a prediction.

Figures can change after their date

Every figure has a source and a date, and can change after it.

Read more

Every figure on the site carries its source and the date it was checked. After that date it can change: index lists are revised every month, fund prices every day, small savings rates every quarter, and rulings can be revisited. Past returns, like those on the fund comparison, don't tell you future returns. Check a figure against its source, which is linked, before acting on it.

When a mistake is found, it is fixed and recorded in the corrections log. To report one, use the feedback form.

Outside links are not endorsements

A link to another site is not an endorsement.

Links to other sites — fund houses, NSE, government pages, fatwas, YouTube, the newsletter service — are there so you can check a source or reach a service. A link doesn't mean Purevesting endorses that site, its products or its views, and Purevesting has no say over what those sites publish or do.
