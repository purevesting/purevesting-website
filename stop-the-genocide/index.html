Stop the genocide

In September 2025 a UN commission of inquiry concluded that Israel has committed genocide in Gaza. Boycott campaigns ask people not to buy from companies tied to it. This page lists the companies and brands in Tech for Palestine's open boycott dataset — with the dataset's own reasons and sources — so you can check what you buy.

Moin Ali Syed
Founder, Purevesting

Whose list this is. Every entry below is listed by Tech for Palestine: each shows the company and brand names, the reasons the dataset tags it with, and the sources it cites. For Tech for Palestine's full wording on each entry, read their dataset. Purevesting has not investigated each one and adds no judgement of its own. The dataset was last updated in February 2024, so some entries may be out of date — open the sources, and write to salam@purevesting.com about anything that has changed.
