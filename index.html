How much of the market passes

204 of the 501 companies in the Nifty 500 pass NSE's Shariah screen.

Each square is one company, and filled squares pass. By market value, each square is an equal slice of the market's value instead.

Over the last 1 and 5 years, NSE's Shariah index has done worse than the Nifty 500 (to 30 September 2026). The two hold different businesses: banks, insurers and lenders, the Nifty 500's biggest sector, are almost absent from the Shariah index. See both, side by side.

  • The 204, the 501 and the 31%: Nifty500 Shariah and Nifty 500 factsheets, NSE Indices — as of 30 September 2026. The 31% is worked out from Infosys, which is 1.83% of the Nifty 500 and 5.83% of the Shariah index: both indices weight each company by the value of its freely traded shares, so 1.83 ÷ 5.83 is the Shariah index's share of the whole.
