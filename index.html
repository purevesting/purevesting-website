Don't compromise on ethics

All of your money, not just the stock market — mutual funds, EPF, PPF, gold, insurance, and the debt you carry. This site shows the working, names the Shariah authority behind every position, and puts a source and a date on every figure.

204 of the 501 companies in the Nifty 500 pass NSE's Shariah screen.

Each square is one company in the Nifty 500. Filled squares pass. Squares are not in index order. In the market-value view, each square stands for an equal slice of the index's value instead.
By market value, the companies that pass come to about 31% of the Nifty 500. That is worked out from Infosys, which is 1.83% of the Nifty 500 and 5.83% of the Shariah index: both indices weight each company by the value of its freely traded shares, so 1.83 ÷ 5.83 is the Shariah index's share of the whole.
Sources: Nifty500 Shariah and Nifty 500 factsheets, NSE Indices — as of 30 September 2026.

Over 1 year and 5 years the Nifty500 Shariah index is behind the Nifty 500, as of 30 September 2026. The two hold different businesses: financial services, the Nifty 500's biggest sector, all but disappear from the Shariah index. See both indices, with sources.
