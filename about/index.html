About Purevesting

Purevesting is a startup building India's one-stop destination for halal finance: investing, saving and borrowing. It answers one question for Muslims in India — what to actually do with your money — across all of it, not just the stock market.

What it covers

All of your money: the shares and mutual funds you choose, the EPF and NPS that come with a job, the PPF and Sukanya Samriddhi accounts people open to save tax, the gold in the family, and the interest hidden in loans and EMIs.

It started in July 2024 as a YouTube channel explaining halal investing in Hinglish. This site, which went live in September 2026, is where every answer is written down in full, with its sources.

How every page is made

Every figure has a source and a date

From a document you can open, in the Sources at the bottom of each page. Tap a figure to see its source.

Every Shariah answer comes from scholars

Quoted in their own words, with a link to the fatwa itself in the page's Sources.

Mistakes are fixed in public

Each fix goes in the corrections log with the date and what changed — nothing is quietly edited.
