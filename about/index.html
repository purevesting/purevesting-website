What Purevesting covers

Most halal investing advice stops at the stock market. Purevesting covers the rest of your money too: the shares and mutual funds you choose, the EPF and NPS that come with a job, the PPF and Sukanya Samriddhi accounts people open to save tax, the gold in the family, and the interest hidden in loans and EMIs.

It started in July 2024 as a YouTube channel explaining halal investing in Hinglish. This site, which went live in September 2026, is where every answer is written down in full, with its sources.

How every page is made

Every figure has a source and a date

Each number on the site sits next to the document it came from and the day it was checked. Tap a figure on any page to see them.

Every Shariah position has a name on it

Positions are quoted from the body or scholar that holds them, with a link to the ruling itself. Purevesting does not issue rulings, and does not choose between the ones it quotes.

Never a recommendation

Tables show data in a fixed order and never rank. Purevesting is not registered with SEBI as a research analyst or investment adviser, so nothing here says what to buy, sell or hold.

Mistakes are fixed in public

When something turns out to be wrong, the fix is recorded in the corrections log with the date and what changed — nothing is quietly edited.
