Halal mutual funds in India, compared

India has six mutual funds built on a Shariah screen. Four are checked by a Shariah board: Tata Ethical, Taurus Ethical, Quantum Ethical and the UTI Nifty500 Shariah Index Fund. Of the other two, The Wealth Company does its own screening, and Nippon says its Shariah ETF is not Shariah compliant.

Moin Ali Syed
Last updated 6 November 2026

NSE's Shariah index and the wider market

Over both 1 year and 5 years, NSE's Nifty500 Shariah index is behind the Nifty 500. The two hold very different businesses: banks, insurers and lenders make up about 31% of the Nifty 500 and almost none of the Shariah index, which leans on IT and healthcare instead. A fund that tracks a Shariah index gets whatever that difference brings.
