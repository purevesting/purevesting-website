"Halal" means the company is in NSE's Shariah index: at the last monthly review, its business, its debt, its interest income and its cash were all within the Shariah limits. How the screen works.

Answers can change at each monthly review. A halal company can still earn a little interest, which you give away: the purification calculator works it out.
