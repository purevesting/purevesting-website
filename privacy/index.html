Privacy, in plain words

Purevesting has no accounts and no adverts, the site's own code sets no cookies, and nothing about you is ever sold.

Last updated 8 November 2026

In short

  • You share something about yourself only when you choose to: a question, feedback, or a newsletter sign-up.
  • Visits are counted as totals, without cookies.
  • Pages you open are kept on your own phone, so they still work offline. Those copies never leave it.
  • Nothing about you is ever sold.

What leaves your phone, and why

Questions and feedback

Only what you type in the Ask forms, sent through Web3Forms to salam@purevesting.com, so it can be read and answered.

Read more

The two forms on the Ask a question page send what you type: your question or feedback and, only if you fill them in, your name, your email address and the page you mean.

They go through Web3Forms, a form service, which emails them on to Purevesting's inbox, salam@purevesting.com. That is so your message can be read and, if you left an email address, answered. A good question may be answered in public — in a video, the newsletter or on a page — but never with your name.

If you write to salam@purevesting.com directly, your message and address sit in the same inbox, and are used only to reply.

The newsletter

Your email address, given to Beehiiv when you sign up, to send you one email a week.

Read more

The sign-up box on the newsletter page is the form of Beehiiv, the service that sends HalalEdge. It takes your email address, to send you the newsletter: one email a week, with an unsubscribe link in every issue.

The box loads from Beehiiv's servers, so Beehiiv also sees that you opened that page. Beehiiv offers a second script that tracks where each sign-up came from; Purevesting leaves it out on purpose. What Beehiiv keeps is set out in Beehiiv's privacy policy.

Video pictures

Fetched from YouTube's image server, which sees your internet address. No YouTube player loads.

Read more

The home page and the videos page show a picture for each video, and some other pages show one for a video on the same topic — the screener, the halal stocks, methodology, mutual funds, NPS and digital gold pages, and the purification calculator. Your browser fetches those pictures from YouTube's image server, i.ytimg.com. Like any server, it receives your internet (IP) address and your browser's details with the request.

The pictures are there so you can see which video each card opens. No YouTube player is loaded on the site, and nothing plays unless you tap a card, which opens YouTube itself. YouTube is part of Google: Google's privacy policy.

Delivering pages and counting visits

Cloudflare hosts the site, and counts visits as totals, without cookies.

Read more

Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host, its servers see each request — your internet address and the address of the page — so that they can deliver the page.

A search typed into the screener box on the home page travels to the screener page as part of that address (for example purevesting.com/screener/?q=infosys). The search itself runs on your phone, against the list inside the page; Purevesting keeps no record of it.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits: which pages are read, which site a visit came from, and the country, device and browser, as totals. It uses no cookies, stores nothing on your phone and doesn't single out individual visitors (Cloudflare's own description). The counts show which pages people use, so the site can get better. Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What stays on your phone

Pages saved on your phone

Copies of the pages you open, kept in your browser so they work offline. Never sent anywhere.

Read more

Once you have visited, the site keeps copies of the pages you open, with its own style, script, font and image files, in your browser's storage on your phone or computer. If you install the app, it uses the same copies. They are there so those pages still open without internet, and open faster.

The copies stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. To delete them, clear this site's data in your browser's settings, or uninstall the app.

Calculators, searches and share images

Worked out on your phone, and never sent anywhere.

Read more

What you type into the SIP, loan and purification calculators, and into the search on the screener page itself, is worked out on your phone and never sent anywhere.

"Share as image" draws its picture on your phone and hands it to your phone's share menu (on a computer, it downloads). It isn't uploaded anywhere.

Nothing sold, and your say

Asking for deletion

Email salam@purevesting.com with what to delete.

Read more

Email salam@purevesting.com and say what to delete — a question or feedback you sent, or your address on the newsletter list — with the name or email address you used, so it can be found. It will be deleted from Purevesting's inbox and, for the newsletter, from Beehiiv's list of subscribers. To stop the newsletter, the unsubscribe link in any issue works at any time.

Nothing is sold

Purevesting doesn't sell, rent or trade anything about you. There are no adverts on the site and no advertising trackers.

Links to other sites

A link to another site — YouTube, Instagram, Beehiiv, NSE, a fund house — takes you to that site, and its own privacy policy applies there.

When this page changes

If the site starts using another service, it will be added here, and the "Last updated" date at the top will change. What the site promises about its content is on the disclaimer page.
