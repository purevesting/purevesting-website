What is the nisab today?

The value of 612.36 g of silver. Zakat is due once your wealth reaches it.

Silver or gold?

Scholars differ on which minimum to use for money. Many fatwas in India use silver: a lower minimum means more help reaches the poor. Some scholars use gold, because silver has lost much of its value. Ask a scholar you trust.

With your jeweller's rate

Your jeweller's silver rate for 1 gram × 612.36 is your nisab. By gold: the 24K rate for 1 gram × 87.48.

Sources
  • The gold 999 (24K) and silver 999 rates: IBJA's daily rates (India Bullion and Jewellers Association), the closing (PM) rates of 20 November 2026. IBJA gives gold for 10 g and silver for 1 kg: they are divided by 10 and by 1,000 here.
  • The nisab, 612.36 g of silver or 87.48 g of gold: Fatwa 1758/B=399/TB=1433, on the tola and the nisab. Fatwa 743/741/L=07/1437 uses the same 612.36 g.
  • Gold only: the gold minimum. Gold with cash, or cash only: the silver minimum, as it is better for the poor: Fatwa 867/867/M=09/1437, on which nisab to use.
  • Silver or gold for money: the fatwa just above uses silver. An answer on the nisab of paper money uses whichever minimum is better for the poor: today, silver. A fatwa on the nisab of bank notes prefers gold, because scholars agree on the weight of the gold minimum but differ on the silver one.
  • Which rate to use: Fatwa 1091/953/D=9/1439. The market rate is better, as a precaution; the price a jeweller pays for old gold also fulfils it.
