What this does and doesn't tell you

The return is a guess, not a promise

The box starts at 5.52% a year: what the Nifty500 Shariah index returned a year over the last five years. That is a past return, not a forecast. No fund promises a return, and past returns don't decide future ones. Try other numbers too.

A SIP is a way of buying, not a product

A SIP is a standing instruction to buy units of a fund every month. The Shariah questions are about the fund it buys — which is why India's six Shariah funds are compared side by side, with their real past returns and their sources.

Costs come off every year

Every fund charges an expense ratio, taken from your money each year, and gains can be taxed. Both make the real result smaller than this page shows.

Purification is separate

Shariah-screened funds can still earn a little interest, which many investors give away. The purification calculator works that out.
